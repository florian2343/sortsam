Help & contact

Does Sortsam delete photos automatically?

No. Sortsam only suggests. What you want to remove goes into the review queue; nothing is deleted until you and iOS confirm.

I deleted something by mistake

Deleted photos stay in the Photos app under “Recently Deleted” for 30 days and can be restored there.

Why does the app need access to all photos?

To find similar photos and duplicates the app has to compare your library. This happens only on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded.

The first analysis takes a while

Large libraries, especially with many iCloud-only photos, take a while the first time. Keep the app open and the iPhone charging. Afterwards only new photos are analysed.

Restore purchases

In the app, tap the gear (Settings) at the top right, then “Restore purchases” under “Pro”. Manage subscriptions in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
