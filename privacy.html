Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

In short

Sortsam collects no personal data. Your photos and everything the app derives from them never leave your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics or advertising service, and no tracking.

Access to your photos

Sortsam asks for access to your photo library to find similar photos, screenshots, videos and technically poor shots. All analysis (e.g. image comparison, text recognition in screenshots) happens only on your device; results are stored locally in the app. Text recognised in screenshots is not stored; the app keeps only figures (such as the number of lines) and a one-way fingerprint to recognise exactly identical screenshots. Only items you confirm are deleted, and iOS asks again before deleting.

iCloud Photos

If photos are stored only in iCloud, iOS loads small thumbnails through Apple's Photos framework when the app shows them. For analysis, the app downloads photos from iCloud only if you allow it in Settings. These transfers happen between your device and Apple; we receive no data.

Stored on your device

Analysis results, your decisions and settings stay inside the app on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no payment or account data, only Apple's confirmation whether a purchase is valid.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer it (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The mailbox is hosted by the German email provider mail.de. We delete requests once they are settled and no legal retention duty applies.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are forwarded to us by email via the service Web3Forms (web3forms.com). This may involve a transfer to countries outside the EU. We use these details only to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once it is settled. If you prefer, simply email us directly.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the site and prevent abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified. We use no cookies and no analytics. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Where processing is based on our legitimate interest, you may object to it at any time under Art. 21 GDPR. Apart from emails you send us, we store no data about you, so there is usually nothing to disclose – feel free to contact sortsam.app@mail.de.
