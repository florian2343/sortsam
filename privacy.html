Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

In short

Sortsam collects no personal data. Your photos and everything the app derives from them never leave your iPhone. There is no account, no analytics or advertising service, and no tracking.

Access to your photos

Sortsam asks for access to your photo library to find similar photos, screenshots, videos and technically poor shots. All analysis (e.g. image comparison, text recognition in screenshots) happens only on your device; results are stored locally in the app. Text recognised in screenshots is not stored, only used for classification. Only items you confirm are deleted, and iOS asks again before deleting.

iCloud Photos

If photos are stored only in iCloud, the app can, if you allow it, load small previews through Apple's Photos framework. This transfer happens between your device and Apple; we receive no data.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We receive no payment or account data, only Apple's confirmation whether a purchase is valid.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the site and prevent abuse. We use no cookies and no analytics. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we store no data about you, there is usually nothing to disclose – feel free to contact [KONTAKT-E-MAIL].
